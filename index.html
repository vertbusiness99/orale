Spanish fades a little more with every generation

  1. Almost all Latino immigrants speak Spanish well.
  2. 57% of U.S.-born Latinos can hold a conversation in it.
  3. 34% of the grandkids of immigrants and later generations can.

Source: Pew Research Center, survey of U.S. Latino adults, August 2022.

Órale is here to bring it back. Por y para nuestra gente: built by a Oaxacan-Mexican kid from Santa Ana who understood more than he could say.

What you'll learn

Swipe to see everything Órale covers.

  • Regional Spanish

    How people talk across Mexico, from Monterrey to Yucatán, and in the US.

  • Slang

    What your primos are saying right now, and when not to say it.

  • Speaking

    Practice out loud in private. No one's judging: record yourself and get feedback on your pronunciation.

  • Real conversations

    The taquería, the family party, meeting the in-laws.

  • Spanglish

    When and how people mix Spanish and English, and how to do it naturally.

  • Culture and history

    The stories behind the words, from Nahuatl and Zapotec to the street.

  • Listening

    Real Mexican voices at real speed, from slow to full speed.

  • Vocabulary

    The words people use most, first. Every word comes with audio and a real example.

  • Reading

    Short stories, texts and memes from Mexico, at your level.

  • Writing

    Texting, messages and writing to family, the way people really write.

  • Grammar

    Short, clear explanations when you need them. No textbook drills.
